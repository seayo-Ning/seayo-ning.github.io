# About Me

I am currently a 2nd-year M.S. student in Mechanical Engineering at the School of Mechanical Engineering, <a href="https://www.gxu.edu.cn/"><em class="entity-mention" style="--entity-color: #0F4C92"><img class="entity-logo" src="/images/logos/gxu.png" alt="GXU">Guangxi University</em></a>, located in Nanning, China.

My research interests focus on **deep learning-based intelligent fault diagnosis, machinery condition monitoring, and prognostics and health management (PHM)**. Specifically, my current work investigates deep representation learning for vibration signals, cross-condition / zero-shot fault diagnosis under data-scarce industrial scenarios, and intelligent maintenance for rotating machinery (such as bearings and gearboxes).

🤝 **Collaboration**: I am always open to academic discussions and collaborations related to intelligent fault diagnosis, industrial AI, and deep learning for mechanical systems. Feel free to reach out to me via email at [wbn0826@qq.com](mailto:wbn0826@qq.com)! 🌱
